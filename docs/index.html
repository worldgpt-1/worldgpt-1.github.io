Overview

Learns from everything that moves.Teaches everything that moves.

WorldGPT is one world action model, trained on cars, robot arms, dual-arm robots, humanoids, drones and boats together. From about a second of camera input and an instruction, it imagines how the world will unfold and plans how to act in it, while seeing in depth and segmentation and reasoning in language. It is built as a teacher: one large model in the cloud, distilled into a smaller on-device student for each embodiment. To our knowledge it is the first model to do all of this across land, water and air.

Why one model

One teacher.
Every embodiment learns from it.

Building a separate model for every embodiment multiplies the cost and splits the data. WorldGPT takes two steps instead.

01Learn together

Actions can’t be shared across embodiments: a steering angle means nothing to a robot arm, and a joint angle means nothing to a drone. Pixels can. Every embodiment sees the world through a camera, so pixels are the bridge between them. WorldGPT learns from all of them in that shared pixel space, so each embodiment’s data also teaches the others. That makes far better use of the data than separate models can, and what WorldGPT learns on the road, in the air and on the water carries over to the rest.

02Distill to each

The teacher stays in the cloud and is built to be distilled into a smaller on-device student for each embodiment. A new embodiment adds its data to the teacher instead of starting a new stack.

Capabilities

Perceive. Reason. Imagine. Act.
In one model.

Physical AI is usually built as a pipeline: one model to see, one to reason, a simulator to predict and a policy to act, each built for a single embodiment. WorldGPT replaces the pipeline with one model, across six embodiments.

Perceive

Depth, segmentation and masks for objects you name, on every camera, for the frames it sees and the future it imagines.

Reason

Explains itself: answers questions about the scene and gives the reason behind its decisions, in plain language.

Imagine

Predicts what happens next: seconds of multi-camera video, consistent across views, from about one second of real input. Change the instruction and the future changes.

Act

Trajectories for cars, actions for arms and hands, motion for drones and boats, all from the same model.

Controllable futures

Change the instruction.
Change the future.

A world model is only useful for planning if its futures respond to what you ask. In each example, every future starts from the same real second with the same random seed; only the instruction is different. WorldGPT follows each one, and explains its plan.
